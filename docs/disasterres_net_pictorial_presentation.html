04 · METHODOLOGY · SPATIAL M2 & CONTINUOUS DDM

Spatial Localization M2 & Continuous Saliency

Extracting debris contours & continuous damage metrics without GPS metadata.

Because 94% of social disaster media lacks EXIF coordinates, standard models fail to pinpoint hazards. DisasterRes-Net extracts spatial damage footprints directly from visual saliency gradients $S(x, y)$ using adaptive Otsu thresholding $T^*$.

1. DAMAGE EXTENT RATIO: $$R_{\text{damage}} = \frac{A_{\text{debris}}}{A_{\text{frame}}}$$
2. CONTINUOUS DEBRIS METRIC (DEM-100): $$\mathrm{DEM}_{100} = \min\left(100, 100 \times (0.6 R_{\text{damage}} + 0.4 \bar{I}_{\text{debris}})\right)$$
OPERATIONAL VALUE: Delivers polygon bounding coordinates $[x, y, w, h]$ to GIS routing algorithms without requiring sensor telemetry.

05 · METHODOLOGY · SAFETY GATES

Multi-Task Heads & The Confidence Safety Gate

Decoupled classification with Mahalanobis out-of-distribution rejection.

Automating crisis decisions without confidence verification is hazardous. DisasterRes-Net feeds the 2,036-D vector into decoupled multi-task heads protected by a 3-tier geometric safety gate:

1. TOP-2 MARGIN CHECK: $$\Delta = p_{(1)} - p_{(2)} < 0.16 \implies \text{UNCERTAIN FLAG}$$
2. MAHALANOBIS OOD DISTANCE: $$D_M(\mathbf{x}) = \sqrt{(\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu})^T \boldsymbol{\Sigma}^{-1} (\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu})} > \tau$$
3. TEMPERATURE SCALING: Calibrated softmax logits reduce ECE from 14.5% to 4.1%.
