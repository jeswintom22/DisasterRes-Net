02 · The Crisis Triage Dilemma

The Information Bottleneck in Emergency Operations

Skew > 90:1

Minority Hazard Blindness

Social imagery is dominated by non-disaster posts and frequent hurricanes. Critical rare perils like landslides (490 scenes) and floods (1,303) are silenced, yielding an unacceptable 2.08% baseline flood recall.

ECE > 14%

Uncalibrated Overconfidence

Standard deep neural networks output high confidence (90%+) on incorrect or ambiguous scenes. Classifiers lack temperature scaling, giving emergency dispatchers false certainty on blurry boundaries.

Zero Localization

Spatial Blindness

Image-level labels fail to specify where damage is located. First responders need exact polygon boundaries, affected area footprints, and contiguous rubble centroids to route tactical rescue gear.

CORE PRINCIPLE: In life-critical disaster management, an erroneous forced classification is significantly more costly than an explicit abstention. Gupta & Roy (2024, 2025)

SHIELD 01

64-Bit dHash Deduplication

Social reposts share scene angles. Grayscale 8×9 horizontal gradients generate a 64-bit binary hash. Cross-split pairs with Hamming distance ≤ 4 are purged, preventing visual memorization.

SHIELD 02

Blank Banner & Quality Guard

Scraped images often include solid-color banners or corrupt headers. An automated filter evaluates RGB standard deviation $\sigma_{\text{rgb}}$; images with $\sigma < 2.0$ are quarantined before GPU passes.

SHIELD 03

Bicubic Standardization

Images are standardized to $299 \times 299 \times 3$ with bicubic interpolation, fitting Inception-ResNet-V2 receptive fields. Pixel values are mapped to $[0, 1]$ prior to LMS opponent processing.

SHIELD 04

Zero-Leakage Normalization

`StandardScaler` parameters ($\boldsymbol{\mu}, \boldsymbol{\sigma}$) are fitted strictly within training folds during cross-validation, guaranteeing zero leakage of test distributions.

Biological LMS Saliency Transformation

Transforms RGB into human cone response space: $RG = L - M$, $BY = S - (L + M)/2$, and Luminance $I = (L + M + S)/3$. Multi-scale center-surround pyramids isolate structural collapses that convolutional pooling often blends with vegetation.

Why Hybrid Fusion Outperforms Pure CNNs (+6.8%)

Deep CNNs capture macro-geometry but smooth over fine surface granularity. GLCM energy isolates floodwater specular reflection, while LBP entropy captures shattered building rubble, boosting macro F1 by +6.8% over deep features alone.
