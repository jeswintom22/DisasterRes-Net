01 · INTRODUCTION & RESEARCH GAPS

The Information Bottleneck in Emergency Operations

In catastrophic disasters, rescue operations face a critical 24-hour golden window where survival odds drop exponentially. While citizens and drones upload thousands of ground-level crisis photos every hour, manual inspection takes 4–8 hours per sector. Meanwhile, off-the-shelf computer vision algorithms fail first responders due to three fundamental vulnerabilities:

Data Skew > 90:1 Gap 01

Minority Hazard Blindness

Why it occurs: Datasets are flooded with harmless photos and frequent hurricanes. Standard models optimize for naive accuracy by always predicting the majority class.

Operational Danger: Near-zero sensitivity: Baseline models yielded 2.08% flood recall and 7.14% landslide recall, completely abandoning trapped victims.
ECE > 14% Gap 02

Uncalibrated Overconfidence

Why it occurs: Deep neural networks output uncalibrated softmax scores. Models report 90%+ certainty even on blurry, mislabeled, or misleading scenes.

Operational Danger: False certainty: Commanders waste limited helicopters and rescue boats on false alarms based on uncalibrated algorithmic overconfidence.
No Coordinates Gap 03

Spatial Blindness & Forced Guesses

Why it occurs: Models assign a single label to an entire image without locating damage polygons, and lack an 'uncertain' state to abstain on irrelevant memes or corrupt inputs.

Operational Danger: Tactical void: First responders cannot deploy gear without knowing where bridges or levees broke within the visual frame.
⚖️ CORE AXIOM: In life-critical disaster management, an erroneous forced classification is significantly more costly than an explicit abstention.

Biological LMS Saliency Transformation

Transforms RGB into human cone response space: $RG = L - M$, $BY = S - (L + M)/2$, and Luminance $I = (L + M + S)/3$. Multi-scale center-surround pyramids isolate structural collapses that convolutional pooling often blends with vegetation.

Why Hybrid Fusion Outperforms Pure CNNs (+6.8%)

Deep CNNs capture macro-geometry but smooth over fine surface granularity. GLCM energy isolates floodwater specular reflection, while LBP entropy captures shattered building rubble, boosting macro F1 by +6.8% over deep features alone.

SHIELD 01

64-Bit dHash Deduplication

Social reposts share scene angles. Grayscale 8×9 horizontal gradients generate a 64-bit binary hash. Cross-split pairs with Hamming distance ≤ 4 are purged, preventing visual memorization.

SHIELD 02

Blank Banner & Quality Guard

Scraped images often include solid-color banners or corrupt headers. An automated filter evaluates RGB standard deviation $\sigma_{\text{rgb}}$; images with $\sigma < 2.0$ are quarantined before GPU passes.

SHIELD 03

Bicubic Standardization

Images are standardized to $299 \times 299 \times 3$ with bicubic interpolation, fitting Inception-ResNet-V2 receptive fields. Pixel values are mapped to $[0, 1]$ prior to LMS opponent processing.

SHIELD 04

Zero-Leakage Normalization

`StandardScaler` parameters ($\boldsymbol{\mu}, \boldsymbol{\sigma}$) are fitted strictly within training folds during cross-validation, guaranteeing zero leakage of test distributions.

11 · CONCLUSION & RESEARCH IMPACT

DisasterRes-Net: Core Architectural & Empirical Breakthroughs

1. Multimodal 2,036-D Fusion Contract

Unifying dual-stream Inception-ResNet-V2 deep CNN embeddings, LMS color-opponent saliency attention, 4-angle GLCM, and uniform LBP moments outperforms single-backbone deep models by +6.8% Macro F1, capturing fine structural rubble and water turbulence.

2. Life-Critical Minority Sensitivity

Class-balanced subsampling and out-of-fold probability multipliers completely eliminate minority peril blindness, triggering a 20× recall surge on floods (2.08% → 41.0%) and a 6× surge on landslides (7.14% → 41.0%) with zero collapse of non-disaster precision.

3. Statistical Calibration & Safety Guards

Temperature scaling ($T=0.427$) drops Expected Calibration Error by -71.7% (0.1453 → 0.0411). Top-2 margin abstention ('uncertain' state) and Mahalanobis OOD distance guards eliminate dangerous overconfidence on ambiguous or out-of-domain imagery.

CrewAI operational desk unites real-time GDACS / UN OCHA event polling, multi-vendor satellite scraping, and continuous DEM-100 spatial density maps (DDM), backed by a strict numeric fidelity regex gate with zero hallucination tolerance.
